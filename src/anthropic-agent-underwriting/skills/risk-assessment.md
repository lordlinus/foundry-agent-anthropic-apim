# Underwriting Risk Assessment Skill

Evaluates risk metrics for insurance and credit underwriting.

## Core Evaluation Pillars
1. **Financial Health & Capacity**: Debt-to-Income (DTI), Liquidity Ratios, Operating Margin, Annual Revenue.
2. **Loss History & Exposure**: Prior claims history, loss runs, hazard class, geographic exposure.
3. **Medical & Demographics** (Life/Health): Age, pre-existing conditions, lifestyle factors, occupational hazards.

## Output Structure
- **Risk Score**: 1-100 scale (Low, Moderate, High, Severe).
- **Key Risk Drivers**: Top factors impacting the evaluation.
- **Recommended Premium Adjustment / Debit Points**: Specific adjustments based on risk tier.
