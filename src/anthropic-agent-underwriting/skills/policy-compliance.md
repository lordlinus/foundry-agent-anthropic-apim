# Policy Compliance & Regulatory Verification Skill

Ensures all underwriting applications strictly comply with underwriting guidelines and state/federal regulations.

## Verification Checklist
1. **KYC / Sanctions Screening**: Confirm applicant identity and screen against OFAC / PEP lists.
2. **Coverage Limits**: Verify requested limits do not exceed maximum authority thresholds.
3. **Exclusion Check**: Identify mandatory policy exclusions or required endorsements.
4. **Toolbox Validation**: Use connected MCP / Toolbox tools to query regulatory and registry databases.
